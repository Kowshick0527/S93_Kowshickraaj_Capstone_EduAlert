# EduAlert – Early Academic Warning System

## Project Idea

EduAlert is an Early Academic Warning System designed to help college students identify academic problems before they become serious. The system monitors important academic indicators such as attendance, assessment marks, assignments, and participation and provides early warnings when a student shows signs of declining performance.

## Project Description

Students often realize that their academic performance is declining only after examination results are released. Faculty members also need to monitor different academic indicators separately, making it difficult to identify students who need support at the right time.

EduAlert provides a centralized platform where students can monitor their academic performance and faculty members can identify students who may be at risk. The system provides alerts and supports timely academic intervention.
